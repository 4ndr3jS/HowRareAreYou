Methodology: Traits are assumed statistically independent (see chain above for how each multiplies in). Height rarity uses the two tailed probability of being at least as far from the mean as you are, so both tall and short are rare, and average height is common. Birthday rarity uses real seasonal birth frequency data rather then a flat 1/365. Country sets the reference population but does not change per trait rarity. Gender is a population split, not a rarity score.
